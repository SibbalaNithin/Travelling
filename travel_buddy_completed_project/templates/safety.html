SAFETY BEFORE THE SCENERY

Look out for
one another.

Travel Buddy helps people discover compatible trips. Good judgement and practical safety steps still matter.

✓

Before you agree

  • Review the profile and ask questions.
  • Confirm route, departure time, stops, seats, and cost sharing.
  • Tell a trusted person your itinerary.
◎

When you meet

  • Meet somewhere public and well-lit.
  • Trust your instincts; you can decline or leave.
  • Keep your phone charged and transport options in mind.
♡

Protect your privacy

  • Do not share passwords, OTPs, or financial credentials.
  • Share live location using a trusted device/app if desired.
  • Do not treat a profile or review as proof of identity.

Need urgent help?

This portfolio demo does not dispatch emergency services, verify identity, or transmit live GPS. In an emergency, contact local emergency services directly and alert someone you trust. In India, the pan-India emergency number is 112.
